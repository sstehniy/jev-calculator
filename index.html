A calculator that chooses.

Jev sees your expression and fixed choices for the magnitude, sign, and each digit. It answers these together. The app keeps several likely combinations, then asks Jev to choose the answer. Tricky ones get a second look.

Results are rounded to your selected decimal places. The search expands automatically for larger answers. Use ^ for powers and % for percent.

This is Jev’s best guess, with its own probability beside it. Sometimes it nails it. Sometimes it’s hilariously wrong. A separate math check reveals the real result and marks the match in green or red, using your selected decimal places. Each step is one gateway call, with several questions answered together.

Price uses gateway-reported cost when available, otherwise an estimate at $0.042 per million input tokens. Failed requests may have unreported charges.
